推广文本：
工作不总是一件接一件。让多个任务同时运行，自动处理重叠时间，记录你真正投入工作的每一分钟。现在支持灵动岛、实时活动与轻量提醒。

-------------------------------------

中文描述：
时流是一款为真实工作方式设计的轻量工作计时器。

它把每日任务和时间记录放在一起，但不要求你按照固定的番茄时间工作，也不会把简单的计时变成复杂的项目管理。

开始做事时点一下计时，需要暂停、切换或完成时再操作即可。一个任务可以分多次完成，多个任务也可以同时进行。

【多个任务，同时计时】

AI Agent 还在运行，你已经开始修改另一段代码；
文件仍在处理，你同时开始整理内容；
一个任务等待结果时，你继续推进另一件事。

时流允许这些任务同时保持计时，不需要为了开始下一件事而强行结束上一件事。

【重叠时间，不重复计算】

如果两个任务同时运行 30 分钟，你实际经过的工作时间仍然是 30 分钟，而不是 60 分钟。

时流会自动处理重叠的时间，让每天的总投入更接近真实经过的时间。

【每日任务 + 工作计时】

把今天准备做的事情放进任务列表。

开始工作时计时，暂停、继续或完成都由你自己决定。一个任务可以跨多个时间段完成，每天做过的事情和实际投入都会留下记录。

【不强制番茄时间】

没有必须完成的 25 分钟，也没有强制休息。

工作 5 分钟也可以，连续投入 2 小时也可以。时流跟随你的工作节奏，而不是要求你适应计时器。

【灵动岛与实时活动】

任务运行时，可以通过实时活动快速查看当前计时状态，在支持的设备上也可以从灵动岛查看正在进行的任务。

【轻量提醒】

可以设置每天的工作开始提醒。

当一个任务持续运行较长时间时，时流也可以提醒你检查当前状态，避免忘记停止计时，也顺便提醒自己适当休息。

【回看真正投入的时间】

通过最近 7 天和月度统计，查看：

· 每天实际投入了多少时间
· 时间主要花在哪些分类
· 最近完成了哪些事情

【简单、私密】

无需注册账号。

任务、分类、计时记录和自定义分类图标均保存在设备本地。

时流想做的事情很简单：

记录今天做了什么，以及真正花了多少时间。

en 推广文本，小于170字符
Run multiple timers at once without double-counting overlapping time. Track real work with Live Activities and lightweight reminders—no forced Pomodoro sessions.

en介绍
ThreadFlow is a lightweight work timer designed for the way real work actually happens.

It combines daily tasks and time tracking without forcing you into fixed Pomodoro sessions or turning simple time tracking into complex project management.

Start a timer when you begin working. Pause, resume, switch, or finish whenever you want. A task can span multiple sessions, and multiple tasks can run at the same time.

[Run multiple tasks at once]

An AI agent is still running while you start working on something else.

A file is processing while you move on to writing, planning, or another task.

One task is waiting for a result while you continue with the next.

ThreadFlow lets these tasks keep running together. You do not need to stop one timer just to start another.

[No double-counted time]

If two tasks run together for 30 minutes, your actual elapsed work time is still 30 minutes — not 60.

ThreadFlow automatically accounts for overlapping sessions so your daily total better reflects the time that actually passed.

[Daily tasks + work timer]

Add what you want to work on today and start timing with one tap.

Pause, resume, or finish whenever you need to. A task can span multiple sessions, and your completed work stays in your daily history.

[No forced Pomodoro sessions]

There is no required 25-minute focus block and no forced break.

Work for five minutes or two hours. ThreadFlow follows your workflow instead of forcing you into a fixed routine.

[Live Activities]

Keep an eye on active timers through Live Activities and, on supported devices, the Dynamic Island.

Quickly check what is running without returning to the app.

[Lightweight reminders]

Set a reminder to start tracking your work.

ThreadFlow can also remind you when a timer has been running continuously for a long time, helping you check whether it should still be running or whether it is time for a break.

[See where your time went]

Review your actual work time over the last 7 days or by month.

See how much time you spent each day, how your time was distributed across categories, and what you completed.

[Simple and private]

No account is required.

Tasks, categories, timer records, and custom category icons are stored locally on your device.

ThreadFlow is built to answer two simple questions:

What did I work on today?

How much time did I actually spend?
